This project was made for Week 11, Themes of Generative Art. My theme for that week was Visualize, and I followed this tutorial by Patt Vira to generate a visual of Chladni figures, which is a pattern that emerges by vibrating particles on a metal pan. They are also sometimes referred to as Nodal Patterns. This particular animation I made, which is a slight variation on the one that Patt makes, aims to add a sort of wind blowing sand effect into the different shapes and patterns. It can also be interacted with to randomize the pattern into something new.
